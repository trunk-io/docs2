<Warning>Workspaces is currently available in private preview.</Warning>

Trunk Workspaces gives you an isolated cloud development environment with a coding
agent, terminal access, persistent files, and shareable HTTP services.
Effortlessly steer and manage workspaces from your desktop app.

Use workspaces to:

- Start a warm coding environment with your disk caches baked in.
- Work with a coding agent and multiple terminal tabs, or launch them from Slack.
- Share agent chats directly with colleagues, instead of passing markdown files around.
- Save compute cost with fast sleep and resume that stays out of your way.
- Monitor and audit agent access to external services through auditing proxies.
- Secure the environment and bring your own encryption keys to meet your organization's security goals.

A template can carry a **Tailscale tag**. A workspace from that template joins your
organization's tailnet and advertises the tag. Your Tailscale ACLs then decide what
the workspace can reach.

## Set the tag

1. Open the template in **Settings** → **Organization** → **Templates**.
2. In **Tailscale tag**, enter an owned tag, for example `tag:trunk-sandbox`.
3. Save the template.

<Note>
  The **Tailscale tag** field is available only when your organization is
  enrolled in Tailscale. See
  [Tailscale](/trunk-workspaces/access-policy/tailscale) to enroll and to learn
  which tags the OAuth client owns.
</Note>

The app mints a fresh Tailscale key for the tag on every workspace boot.

<Warning>
  Network egress policies are in Beta. The controls and behavior can change.
</Warning>

A network egress policy sets an allowlist of domains that a workspace can reach.
Traffic to a domain outside the list is blocked.

You attach a policy to a template. Every workspace from that template then follows
the policy. This lets you keep a workspace's outbound access to only the services it
needs.

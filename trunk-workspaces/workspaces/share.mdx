A workspace owner shares access from the workspace menu (**☰**) → **Sharing**. A
member you share with sees the workspace under **Shared workspaces** in their
sidebar.

## Share with a member

In **Sharing**, type a name or email in **Add people…** and click the member to
add them. An added member gets access right away. Click the **×** beside a member
to remove them.

## Share with a link

Click **Share org link** in **Sharing** when you want a member to add themselves.
The app copies the link to your clipboard. Use **Copy org link** to copy it again,
or **Turn off org link** to revoke it. The
member must sign in and belong to the workspace's organization. The link adds the
member and opens the workspace for them.

To learn what a shared collaborator can do with the agent, see
[Collaborate](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/cooperation).

<Note>
  A member who already opened a running workspace keeps access until the
  workspace stops. Stop the workspace to end access for a removed member.
</Note>

Workspace owners can share access from **Details** → **Summary**.

## Share with an organization member

Use **Share with** to add or remove members of the selected organization. Added
members see the workspace under **Shared workspaces** in their sidebar.

## Share with a link

Create an organization-shareable link when you want a member to add themselves.
The recipient must sign in and belong to the workspace's organization. Opening the
link adds the recipient and redirects them into the workspace.

Shared viewers can use the **Agent** tab collaboratively and inspect the workspace's
HTTP ports in **Details**. They cannot open a terminal, change lifecycle state,
manage sharing, or change the owner's workspace configuration.

A workspace owner shares access from **Details** → **Summary**. A member you share
with sees the workspace under **Shared workspaces** in their sidebar.

## Share with a member

Use **Share with** to add or remove members of your organization. An added member
gets access right away.

## Share with a link

Create an organization-shareable link when you want a member to add themselves. The
member must sign in and belong to the workspace's organization. The link adds the
member and opens the workspace for them.

To learn what a shared collaborator can do with the agent, see
[Collaborate](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/cooperation).

<Note>
  A member who already opened a running workspace keeps access until the
  workspace stops. Stop the workspace to end access for a removed member.
</Note>

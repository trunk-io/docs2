Workspaces are collaborative. Your team can work with the same agent together, and
you can launch a workspace from Slack or GitHub.

## Work with your team in one agent room

When you share a workspace, the **Agent** tab becomes multiplayer. Several members
of your organization join the same room and work with the same agent.

A collaborator in a shared room can:

- Send prompts to the agent.
- Answer a permission prompt.
- Cancel a queued message.

Only the owner starts a room. A shared collaborator waits for the owner to start
one. A shared collaborator cannot open a terminal, change the workspace lifecycle,
or manage sharing.

[Share a workspace](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/share).

## Launch from Slack

You can start a workspace without opening the app.

Mention **@blaze** in a Slack thread with your request. The app replies in the
thread with a picker for the template, size, region, and agent. Click **Launch
workspace**. The app creates a workspace that you own, with the thread as the first
prompt, and replies with a link to follow along.

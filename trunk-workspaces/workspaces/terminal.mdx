The **Terminal** tab opens a shell in the selected workspace. Terminal sessions live
in the remote sandbox and can reconnect after you navigate away or restart the app.

## Work with tabs

- Create, rename, reorder, and close terminal tabs from the tab strip.
- Drag files into the workspace to upload them at the active terminal's working
  directory.
- Configure whether closing a tab requires confirmation under **Settings** →
  **Appearance** → **Terminal**.
- Move the terminal tab strip to the top or bottom from the same settings page.

On macOS, use `Command+T` to create a terminal tab and `Command+W` to close one.
Use `Shift+Command+W` to close the window. On Windows and Linux, use
`Ctrl+Shift+T` and `Ctrl+Shift+W`; the terminal reserves bare `Ctrl+T` and
`Ctrl+W` for shell applications.

If the workspace is asleep or stopped, the terminal shows a **Wake** or **Resume**
action instead of connecting. Resuming preserves the workspace disk, but a stopped
workspace does not preserve in-memory processes.

Trunk team learns your goals, sets up your organization, and gives your team access.

## What to expect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Book a demo">
    Pick a time with the Trunk team. Tell us about your organization and your
    security requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We set up your organization">
    Trunk creates your organization, prepares your templates, and connects the
    integrations you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your team gets access">
    Trunk grants access to your team members and shares the desktop app
    download.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first workspace">
    Install and open the desktop app, sign in, then launch a workspace. See
    [Launch a workspace](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/home).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Already onboarded? Open the desktop app and sign in, then follow [Launch a
  workspace](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/home).
</Info>

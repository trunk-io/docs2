These instructions assume Trunk has given you access and the trunk desktop app is
already installed.

## Sign in

1. Open the trunk app.
2. Click **Sign In**. The app opens your system browser to complete authentication.
3. Return to the app after the browser confirms the sign-in.
4. Select your organization if your account belongs to more than one.

<Frame caption="Sign in to the trunk app.">
  ![The trunk app sign-in screen with a Sign In
  button.](/assets/trunk-workspaces/sign-in.png)
</Frame>

The desktop app remembers your selected organization. Use the organization menu in
the sidebar when you need to switch.

## Open or create a workspace

If your organization already has a workspace, select it from the sidebar. The app
restores the last tab you used for that workspace and otherwise opens **Terminal**.

If the organization has no workspaces, click **Add workspace** and follow
[Create a workspace](/trunk-workspaces/workspaces/create).

<Frame caption="An organization with no workspaces yet.">
  ![The empty workspace screen with an Add workspace
  button.](/assets/trunk-workspaces/empty-workspaces.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Workspaces under **Shared workspaces** belong to another organization member.
  Shared workspaces expose a smaller set of controls than workspaces you own.
</Info>

An organization Tailscale enrollment lets newly started workspaces join the
organization's tailnet with the configured tags and naming rules.

Navigate to **Settings** → **Organization** → **Tailscale** to inspect the current
enrollment. Organization admins and owners can enroll or disconnect the organization;
other members see the current read-only state.

<Frame caption="Enroll the organization in Tailscale.">
  ![The organization Tailscale settings
  page.](/assets/trunk-workspaces/tailscale-settings.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start enrollment">
    Click the enrollment action and complete the Tailscale authorization in your
    browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the configuration">
    Return to the trunk app and confirm that the organization is connected with
    the expected tailnet and tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a workspace">
    Create or resume a workspace so its boot process receives a fresh Tailscale
    key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Disconnecting the organization prevents later workspace boots from receiving a
  key. It does not serve as an immediate remote kill switch for an
  already-running Tailscale node.
</Note>

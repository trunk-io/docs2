---
title: "Merge queues"
description: "How Dynamic CI behaves when your merge queue tests a pull request."
og:title: "Trunk Dynamic CI and merge queues"
---

Dynamic CI runs a little differently on a merge queue than on a pull request.
On a merge-queue run, it skips a job only when a paths filter you configured
excludes it. With no paths filter, every job runs.

Dynamic CI recognizes merge-queue runs on its own: Trunk Merge Queue
(`trunk-merge/` branches), GitHub merge queue (`merge_group` events and
`gh-readonly-queue/` branches), and Buildkite builds on either branch. You don't
need to filter on the event or the branch in your workflow.

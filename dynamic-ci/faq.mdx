## Reliability

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens if Trunk is slow or down?">
    Every job runs. The action tries up to three times, 30 seconds each, and on
    a timeout or an error it returns no verdicts. The step itself never fails,
    and the fail-open is logged as a warning and written to the job summary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to a job Trunk has never seen?">
    It runs. A job needs a couple of weeks of history before Trunk will
    recommend skipping it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Dynamic CI skip a job in my merge queue?">
    Only when a paths filter you configured excludes it. See
    [Merge queues](/dynamic-ci/merge-queue).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Forks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens on pull requests from forks?">
    A pull request from a fork has no access to your repository's secrets, so
    the filter job has no token and every job runs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

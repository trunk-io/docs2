**You can now flag any test as flaky directly from its detail page, with a single click and an optional reason.**

![Test detail page with the Flag as Flaky control and the flagged banner](/assets/changelog/flaky-tests-flag-as-flaky-one-click-mock.png)

A **Flag as Flaky** button with a flag icon sits in the test status strip. Click it, optionally enter a reason (up to 256 characters), and the test is flagged immediately. An amber banner then shows who flagged it, when, and why, with a one-click option to remove the flag. The events panel records every flag and unflag action in a human-readable Flag History.

This replaces the old "manual monitor" workflow that was buried in the Monitors tab.

[Read the docs](/flaky-tests/detection/flag-as-flaky#flagging-a-test) to learn more.

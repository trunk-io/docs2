**Re-run a failed check without losing your place in the queue.**

When a required check fails, re-run the failed checks instead of resubmitting. The pull request is tested again and keeps its place in the queue.

- **From Trunk.** Click **Re-run failed checks** to re-run failed GitHub Actions jobs, now or when their workflow finishes.
- **From your CI.** Re-run the failed checks yourself, in any CI that reports to GitHub.
- **Opt-in.** Turn on **Accept Manual Re-runs of Failed Required Checks** in your queue's **Settings**, or through the [API](/api/mergequeues/update-a-merge-queues-configuration).

See [Re-run failed checks](/merge-queue/using-the-queue/rerun-failed-checks) for details.

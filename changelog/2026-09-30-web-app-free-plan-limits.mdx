**Free plan limits now match [trunk.io/pricing](https://trunk.io/pricing).**

- **Flaky Tests:** 1 million test spans per committer per month, up to 5 million.
- **Merge Queue:** unlimited PRs merged, for up to 5 committers.
- **Public repositories:** committers are now counted on every repository with Trunk enabled, public or private. Public repositories are no longer free by default.

If you maintain an open source repository, contact [sales@trunk.io](mailto:sales@trunk.io) to talk through how your contributors are counted.

See [Billing and plans](/setup-and-administration/billing) for the full breakdown.

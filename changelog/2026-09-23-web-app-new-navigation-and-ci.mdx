**The Trunk app has a new sidebar, a new CI tab, and a new onboarding flow to help you set it all up.**

### A new sidebar

Product navigation has moved from the top bar to a collapsible sidebar on the left, making room for new products like CI. The sidebar holds the organization switcher, a **Search** row that opens the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> still works), your products, and the organization settings and user menus. Collapse it to icons for more room; Trunk remembers your choice.

### The CI tab

CI brings performance and flaky monitoring to your GitHub Actions workflows. See which jobs are slow, which are failing, and what your CI costs, all in one place.

- **Overview, Failures, Jobs, Logs, Monitors, and Pricing.** Track how your workflows are performing, what's failing, and which jobs are slowest, and set up monitors to catch flaky and failing jobs.
- **See what CI costs.** The **Jobs** table has a **Cost** column, and each job's header shows its cost. The **Pricing** tab lists every runner your organization uses, with its specs and current price. Admins can set custom prices to match what you actually pay.
- **Search your logs.** On **CI → Logs**, filter by **Branch** or **PR**, search for an exact phrase by putting it in quotes, and click any line to open a detail panel with a link to its job.

Support for additional CI providers is coming soon.

### Guided setup

A new onboarding flow makes it easier to get every product running. Instead of an empty page, each product you haven't set up now shows you what to do next:

- **CI** opens on a setup checklist until your first workflow data arrives.
- **Merge Queue** takes you straight to creating your first queue.
- **GitHub App errors are explained.** If installing the Trunk GitHub App is refused, for example because you're signed in to the wrong GitHub account, Trunk tells you why and how to fix it.

Head to **CI** in the sidebar to get started, or read the [CI docs](/ci) and the [GitHub Actions setup guide](/ci/get-started/github-actions).

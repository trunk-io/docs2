Trunk now supports single sign-on (SSO) for [enterprise plan](https://trunk.io/pricing) users. SSO
allows your team to access Trunk with their work email account through your organization's identity
and access management solutions, making it easier to manage your Trunk organization.

![](/assets/changelog/web-app-single-sign-on-sso-support-5qox0y.png)

SSO is only available for [enterprise plan](https://trunk.io/pricing) users. If you're interested in
configuring SSO for your team, contact us by [email](< mailto:support@trunk.io>) or
[Slack](https://slack.trunk.io/). We will reach out to help you configure SSO for your organization.

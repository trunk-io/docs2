## May 18, 2026

<Update label="May 18, 2026" tags={["Flaky Tests", "New feature"]}>
  **[Failure Count Monitor](/changelog/2026-05-18-flaky-tests-failure-count-monitor)**

  Flag tests the moment they accumulate a configured number of failures on monitored branches.
</Update>

<Update label="May 18, 2026" tags={["Flaky Tests", "New feature"]}>
  **[Organization-Scoped Test Labels](/changelog/2026-05-18-flaky-tests-test-labels)**

  Tag, organize, and filter your test suite with org-wide labels.
</Update>

<Update label="May 18, 2026" tags={["Flaky Tests", "Update"]}>
  **[Apply Labels from Monitors](/changelog/2026-05-18-flaky-tests-monitor-label-actions)**

  Monitors can now apply labels to tests instead of classifying them as flaky or broken.
</Update>

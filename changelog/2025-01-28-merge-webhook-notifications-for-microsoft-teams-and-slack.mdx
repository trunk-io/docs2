We're excited to share our new webhook integrations for Trunk Merge. You can now send notifications
about events in the merge queue to your Microsoft Teams and Slack channels.

These webhooks allow you to receive notifications when a PR enters the merge queue, begins testing,
and succeeds or fails in the queue. These webhook come with a template transformation that works out
of the box and can be customized using a JavaScript-based transformation code block.

![](/assets/changelog/merge-webhook-notifications-for-microsoft-teams-and-slack-4bn17t.png)

You can create an integration under **Organization** > **Webhooks** by clicking **Add Endpoint**.
Under **Webhook**, you can select the type of integration for your messaging service. Instructions
are provided in each of the provided integrations.

[Login to Trunk to add a webhook integration](https://app.trunk.io/)

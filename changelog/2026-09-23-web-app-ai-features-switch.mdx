**Admins can now turn AI features on or off for their whole organization.**

Go to **Settings → Organization → General** and use the **Allow AI features** toggle on the **AI Features** card. AI features are on by default, and only organization admins can change the setting. Trunk asks you to confirm before turning them off.

When AI features are off, Trunk stops sending test and CI failure output to AI models, and stops showing AI-generated results:

- **Failure summaries** show the raw failure text.
- **Failure grouping** groups only failures that match exactly.
- **Flaky test investigations** are turned off, including automatic investigations, **Analyze**, and investigations started over MCP.
- **CI failure analysis and job summaries** are turned off.

Where an AI feature would normally appear, the page shows **AI features are turned off**. Turn the switch back on at any time to restore them.

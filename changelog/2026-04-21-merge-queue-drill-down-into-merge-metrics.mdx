**Merge Queue Health metrics can now be drilled down to the individual pull requests behind any data
point.** Previously, the charts on the Health page surfaced aggregate numbers like conclusion counts
and time in queue, but there was no way to jump from a suspicious spike to the PRs that caused it.
Now, you can click any data point — or drag to select a range — and hit **View PRs** to open a
sortable list of the underlying PRs, making it easy to track down p99 outliers or investigate a
cluster of failures.

[Read the Docs](https://docs.trunk.io/merge-queue/administration/metrics#drill-down-into-metrics) to
learn more

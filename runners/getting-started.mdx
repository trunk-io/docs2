This page shows how to run your first job on Trunk Runners.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your organization">
    Install the Trunk GitHub App on your organization. On a demo call, the Trunk team turns on Trunk Runners for you and sets up your access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your defaults">
    Set an organization default template, region, and size. Most jobs then need only a size in the label. See [Organization defaults](/runners/settings/ci-defaults).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point a job at Trunk Runners">
    Change the `runs-on` label of a job to the Trunk Runners label. The label sets the machine size. See [The runs-on label](/runners/actions/runs-on).

    ```yaml
    jobs:
      build:
        runs-on: trunk=${{ github.run_id }}-${{ github.run_attempt }}..size=medium
        steps:
          - uses: actions/checkout@v4
          - run: make build
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the workflow">
    Push a commit or start the workflow. Trunk provisions a fresh VM, runs the job, and removes the VM when the job ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Do you have many workflows to move? Trunk can open a pull request that rewrites your `runs-on` labels for you. Ask the Trunk team. See the [Roadmap](/runners/roadmap).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your organization">
    [Install the Trunk GitHub and Trunk Runners apps](https://app.trunk.io/settings/organization/github)
    on your organization. Then contact Trunk support to add your organization to
    the Trunk Runners Beta program.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your defaults">
    Set an organization default template. Most jobs then need no options
    in the label. See
    [Organization defaults](/runners/settings/ci-defaults).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point a job at Trunk Runners">
    Change the `runs-on` label of a job to a `trunk=` label. With no
    options, the job runs on a machine with 2 CPUs and 8 GiB of memory. See
    [The runs-on label](/runners/actions/runs-on).

    ```yaml
    jobs:
      build:
        runs-on: trunk=${{ github.run_id }}-${{ github.run_attempt }}
        steps:
          - uses: actions/checkout@v4
          - run: make build
    ```

  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the workflow">
    Push a commit or start the workflow. Trunk provisions a fresh VM, runs the
    job, and removes the VM when the job ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>
